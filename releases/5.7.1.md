# 5.7.1

The evidence journal is a rolling window instead of a hard stop.

From Super Awesome Games ticket VGD2A04892DB-34 (2026-11-12). QA 5.7.0 resumed the ticket correctly after a retry: the
failed behavioral run was settled instead of re-blocking. It then stopped with "QA evidence reached 256 observations;
preserve the existing journal and split further validation."

- **Cause.** The journal is keyed by the stage's source and planning identity, so it accumulates across every attempt.
  After 33 attempts it held 256 observations. `GameQaJournal.RecordAsync` refused the 257th, which every command,
  test plan and browser check records. Each later attempt blocked on its first command, and the platform then marked
  the stage `task.repeated_issue` (not retryable).
- **Fix.** The index keeps the newest `WindowSize` (256) observations, and older ones roll out. Their receipts and parts
  stay stored for audit. `Index.Dropped` keeps observation indexes absolute and stable.
  - `read_qa_evidence` on an observation that has rolled out says so, instead of failing.
  - Prompts already use only the newest 24 to 48 observations, so nothing they relied on is lost.
  - The validated report is tracked separately and stays readable.

After deploying, retry the ticket once: the stage is marked non-retryable until a person retries it.
