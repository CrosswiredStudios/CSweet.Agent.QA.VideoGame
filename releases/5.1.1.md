# 5.1.1

Browser testing now refreshes the compute environment after a retained request is submitted without a previously known operation ID. A request rejected or lost before acceptance can advance the environment generation on retry; comparing its operation against the earlier environment snapshot incorrectly blocked QA with a different-operation-identity error.

Recovery preserves the exact request, source/build hashes and idempotency key. Known operations are still read rather than rerun, and environment identity plus generation fences remain enforced. Regression coverage exercises lost submissions both before and after acceptance.

The matching platform must also accept browser-upload scripts within the existing combined 32 KiB guest command budget. Deploy through published releases and Installed Agents; local tests and packages do not replace installed agents.
